# NPC reactions — a table of what people notice

The engine (`npc_reactions.zbr`) makes Sean's 12/19/01 design-notebook
reaction table executable, and the harness (`npc_reactions_test.zbr`)
fences the ways such a system stops feeling real.

## What it is

A pure function. No state, no randomness:

```
reaction(region, npc-type, player-qualities, standing) -> disposition
```

- **Qualities** are the notebook's ten: wisdom, faith, leadership, loyalty,
  perception, humility, honor, courage, kindness, prowess. They run 0–5 with
  3 as ordinary.
- **The table** is the notebook's own 18×10 grid, stored one string per NPC
  type and one character per quality (`@ # + . - = ~`), so the source keeps
  the shape of the page it came from.
- **Scoring** measures *deviation from an ordinary person*, so an average
  stranger is neutral to everybody and the table expresses what each kind of
  person actually notices. Without that baseline, types with many positive
  weights adore every passer-by and the Fool hates the world on sight.
- **Standing** is what has passed between you: favours owed, service, gifts,
  allegiance. Weighted unequally on purpose — obligation outranks payment,
  per the notebook's own example.
- **Fear** is a separate axis (Sean, 2026-07-31): what a person will *tell*
  you is not the same as how much he likes you. Intimidability per type
  (prophets and kings 0; children and servants 3) × the player's menace.
  Fear buys compliance and costs truth; warmth mitigates it.
- **The trace** names the three terms that moved the result. This is the
  load-bearing feature: a score cannot be reviewed, a reason can.

## Regions

A reaction true in Torial need not hold in Pilisia. Regions are stored as
**overlays** — only the cells where a culture differs — for three reasons:
authoring (18×10 per nation would never be finished), review (a nation
becomes legible as its deviations), and safety (untouched cells keep the
base weight, so a region cannot silently rewrite the world).

The **virtue index** sums what a region's people reward across the five
qualities this world treats as virtues. It lets the harness assert claims
the prose has always made — that Pilisia, which fed its firstborn to Ikral,
rewards virtue less than Torial — as properties of the data. The Tuon come
out *equal* to Torial in total while differing in six cells: same sum,
different shape, which is a truer model of a foreign people than a
better/worse axis.

## Proposed next: place-times, and the two shapes of corruption

*Sean's idea, 2026-07-31; recorded here, not built.*

Make the unit a **place-time** rather than a place: "Pilisia, year 500" and
"Pilisia, year 1470" are two named overlays with dates, and the drift
between them **is** the history. Torial's virtue index could then be watched
falling across the Ikral years and recovering after the temple's
purification — generated by what its people came to reward, rather than
asserted by a narrator, and checkable against the prose that tells the same
story.

The application that makes this worth building is the Eldar. Corruption in
this world has (at least) two shapes, and a reaction table distinguishes
them:

| | Appetite-corruption | Coercion-corruption |
|---|---|---|
| **Example** | Pilisia | the first-sending Eldar |
| **Cause** | came to love the wrong things | *"worship this god so that another is not tortured before your eyes"* |
| **Signature** | drift toward the Fool's row: kindness ↓, humility ↓, prowess ↑ | **kindness intact, faith inverted** — the compassion never broke; it was the lever |

That second row also derives the **Senas** honestly. They descend from the
fallen Eldar, and canon makes them sorcerers rather than brutes: their table
should show high wisdom and perception weights with faith and kindness
inverted. Learned, long-lived, and cruel — arrived at from the mechanism
instead of asserted.

## Status

All values in the shipped overlays are **proposals grounded in canon**, not
rulings. Pilisia is drawn from the child-sacrifice to Ikral and the judgment
that its people were "not noble, nor worthy of any praise"; the Tuon from
song, honour, and the defence of cubs. The machinery does not care what the
numbers are — it only insists that each region actually differ, that
overlays stay surgical, and that an ordinary stranger stays neutral
everywhere.

Not yet built: perception (NPCs misjudging your qualities — nuance lives in
imperfect reading), memory of past encounters, the accumulation sweep across
a full playthrough, and place-times as above. The sweep is also the fitness
function any tuning search (genetic or otherwise) would need before it could
exist.
